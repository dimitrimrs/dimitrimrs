## 🟢 About me:
💻 Backend Developer | Full-Stack Enthusiast | Technology & Linux Lover

Welcome!

I’m Dimitri, a passionate Backend Developer currently working at Grupo Sideral, building scalable and efficient systems using Node.js, TypeScript, Fastify, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, Zod, Swagger and Vitest.
In parallel, I contribute as a Full-Stack Developer intern at the Centro de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento de Softwares – UESB, where I work with PHP, Laravel, Vue.js, Tailwind, MySQL and Docker, consolidating my skills in complete web solutions and academic research projects.
I’m also a Linux enthusiast 🐧. My daily driver is Arch Linux with Hyprland and HyDE, but I’ve explored several distributions such as Ubuntu and Zorin, which sharpened my adaptability and curiosity for different development environments.
With a strong analytical mindset, proactive approach, and collaborative spirit, I thrive on solving complex challenges, designing well-structured architectures, and delivering high-quality code.
I’m constantly seeking to improve, innovate, and contribute to impactful software solutions 🚀.

## 💡 Career Highlights:

1. 📱 Contributed to the development of **PesquisaFácil (UESB)**, a mobile application that streamlines the creation, distribution, and analysis of questionnaires in extension projects — participating from initial research and planning through to execution.  
2. ⏳ Developed **Modo Caverna**, a personal productivity and self-development application that supports thousands of users in enhancing focus and time management.  
3. 🏥 Contributed to the implementation of **SGDoctor**, a clinical management system designed for the Physiotherapy Clinic at UESB, improving efficiency in patient and appointment handling.  
4. 🎓 Participated in the development of **SigExt**, a management system for UESB’s extension projects, facilitating administrative control and academic project coordination.  
5. 💇‍♂️ Built scalable sales management systems for **Castelli Professional**, enabling optimized workflows in the commercialization of cosmetic haircare products.  

With a solid track record in designing and delivering reliable solutions, Dimitri brings expertise in **software development, problem-solving, and scalable system design** to drive impactful results in future projects.
